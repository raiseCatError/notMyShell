# Vespyr Run

An endless runner for the Vespyr Arcade (`/game run`, aliases `vespyr-run`, `dino`). Vespyr, the NMSh cat, runs to the
right by himself across a scrolling landscape; the player jumps and ducks. The mechanics are those of the familiar
offline-browser runner; every sprite, obstacle and course is original.

## Controls

| Key | Effect |
|---|---|
| Space or Up | Start the run (from *ready*) and jump. A press in the last 6 steps of a jump is remembered and jumps on touchdown. |
| Down on the ground | Duck for 40 steps (0.67 s); each press restarts the timer, so a held key (which repeats) keeps the duck. A jump leaves the duck. |
| Down in the air | Fast fall: gravity x2.2 for the rest of the jump, then Vespyr lands ducking. |
| p | Pause (shared pause menu). Esc pauses a run, leaves a game not yet started. |

**Variable height without key releases.** The terminal sends presses only, so a "hold to jump higher" is impossible.
The full jump is the default; a *second* press (Down) while airborne cuts it short. Pressing Down right after takeoff
gives a hop under half the airtime and a lower apex; later presses shorten the fall from wherever the cat is.

## Rules and scoring

Hit an obstacle and the run ends (the cat is shown hit for about 0.4 s, 0.13 s with reduced motion, then the shared game
over card with Retry). Score is distance: 1 point per 5 world pixels (2 per meter, 1 m = 10 px), shown as a five digit
leading-zero counter (`typeset({role: 'counter'})`). Passing every 100 points lights the counter for 0.7 s and floats a
number (not with reduced motion). A new best sets `result.record`.

## Physics (`physics.ts`)

Fixed step 1/60 s, semi-implicit Euler, units are world pixels (one canvas column wide, half a row tall; the world is
`columns x 2*rows`). `GRAVITY = 272 px/s^2`, `JUMP_V = 96 px/s` give a 16.2 px apex and 42 steps (0.70 s) of air. The
profile used by every proof (`JUMP_PROFILE`) is generated by the same `airStep` the simulation uses.

Hitboxes (inside the 14 x 8 sprite): 7 wide, standing 7 tall, ducking 4 tall, starting 3 px into the sprite. Only a real
overlap is a hit; touching edges are clear. Aerial obstacles hang with their underside 6 px up: above the ducking cat,
below the standing one, so they must be ducked (jumping into one is a hit).

Speed rises linearly from 48 to 96 px/s over the first 9000 px and then stays at 96 forever (`speedAt`). The cat's
screen position is `14%` of the width (4..24); physics does not depend on the canvas size, so a resize never changes
fairness.

## Course generation and the fairness proof (`course.ts`)

The course is generated ahead of the cat from `rng.fork('course')`, with a fixed number of draws per obstacle (so the
sequence never depends on what the fairness check adjusts). Kinds: stacked `crates`, a single `crate`, `yarn`, garden
`stones` and tin `cans` (ground, 3 to 6 px tall, in clusters of 1 to 3), and `bird` / `plane` (aerial, only after 600 px).
Difficulty (`difficultyAt`, 0 to 1 over the same 9000 px) raises the speed, the share of aerial obstacles
(12% to 38%), the chance and size of clusters and tightens the random extra spacing; everything is nondecreasing and
capped.

`jumpWindow(width, height, speed)` returns the range of takeoff steps from which the real jump profile keeps the feet at
least `height + 1` px above the ground for every step of contact (plus one step either side). A wide thing is hardest
when slow, so a cluster is only used if its window is at least `SLACK_STEPS = 12` (0.2 s) at the slowest and fastest
speed it can be met at. `slot()` turns that into a schedule: a ground obstacle needs a jump started in its window and
keeps the player busy for the whole airtime plus `REACT_STEPS = 12`; an aerial one needs a duck (pressed up to 24
steps before contact, held until it passes) and then `DUCK_REACT_STEPS = 6` before a jump. The next obstacle is
judged against the *latest* start the player could have taken (`SAFE_STEPS = 8` before its window ends), so a player
who acts anywhere inside every window, not only a perfect one, always has `SAFE_STEPS` of slack left for the next.
The generator moves each obstacle right, pixel by pixel, until that holds: the minimum safe gap is found, not guessed.
`minSafeGap(a, b, speed)` exposes it per pair and speed; the worst single-unit pair needs about 40 px at the start and 85 px at the cap (clusters and the random extra spacing add to that), which a normal
terminal shows. Duck-then-jump and jump-then-duck sequences are covered because `slot()` handles both kinds.

## Accessibility

* Without color (NO_COLOR) the backdrop is dropped (every lit pixel would be the same block); the ground is a solid
  `████` band over a textured row, obstacles and the cat are solid shapes with openings (crate slits, eyes, ears).
  With Safe Glyphs the same scene is `#` / `"` / `,` ASCII and the backdrop is also dropped.
* Reduced motion: no screen shake, flash or impact burst, no cloud drift or star twinkle, no milestone popup; the
  counter still lights up. Gameplay motion (scrolling, the cat) is unchanged.
* Effects level scales dust, landing puff, impact burst and popups; `off` draws none.
* Sky and hills come from `skyAt(palette, phase)` (phase advances with distance, a full day/night every 8000 px, stars
  at night); every color derives from the theme palette.
* Canvases under 20 x 8 show `Needs at least 20x8`. Pause is the host's: the game stops receiving steps, its status is
  untouched.

## Record (`arcade.json` -> `run`)

`{best: number (score), bestDistance: number (meters), runs: number}`. Every field is validated on read (finite,
nonnegative, floored, capped at 1e9; anything else reads as 0). A finished run updates all three and counts once;
`persist()` on an unfinished run only raises `best`/`bestDistance`. Hub summary: `Best 420 · 210 m`.

## Tests

`tests/arcadeRunPhysics.test.ts`: jump arc numbers, gravity and landing, jump buffer, duck, fast fall, collision
boundaries (touching vs a hair inside), speed and difficulty ramp and cap, a clearable window for every emitted obstacle
at every speed, schedule verification of every consecutive pair over 120 seeds (thousands of obstacles, including
aerial and clusters), `minSafeGap` per speed and pair against every generated gap, state-hash determinism, and
simulated players on the real world: windows used early, middle, late and at random points survive 20,000 steps on
several seeds at the speed cap, while a player who never acts or only spams jump dies.
`tests/arcadeRunGame.test.ts`: state machine, scoring and the milestone, crash and result, records and corrupt data,
persistence round trip, pause/restart through the session, fresh course per seed, sprite silhouettes and obstacle art
sizes, resize at many sizes and colors, NO_COLOR and Safe Glyphs, reduced motion and effects off.

## Limits

Automated tests are not gameplay verification: feel (jump length, duck hold, the speed curve) and how the half-block
art looks in real terminals and themes need hands-on play. The proof covers a player who acts inside the computed
windows; it does not model key-repeat timing on a given terminal.
